About the Academy

Digital Female Academy (أكاديمية الأُنثى الرقمية) is an educational initiative dedicated to empowering Arabic-speaking women in the world of technology, design, and digital creativity.

Currently, our lessons and content are in Arabic, but soon we’ll launch programs in English and French 🌍.

What We Offer

💻 Courses & Workshops

We teach Web Development, Design, and Digital Tools — all explained clearly in Arabic for all learning levels.

📚 Daily Educational Posts

We share daily tips, lessons, and inspiration to help women stay motivated and creative in the digital world.

🎁 Free Learning Opportunities

We host free mini-courses and interactive live sessions to support our growing community of learners 🌷.

We can learn, work and create together
